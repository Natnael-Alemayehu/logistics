How they work

Each message is a POST of JSON: the event's type, a timestamp, and in data the shipment as the API sends it and what happened to it.

Answer 2xx within 10 seconds. Anything else is tried again after 1 and 5 minutes, half an hour, then 2, 6 and 12 hours. Each webhook's page lists what was sent and how it was answered, for 30 days.

Messages are signed as Standard Webhooks describes, so any of its libraries can check them: see a webhook's page for its secret.
