Quick answers

A driver forgot their PIN
They can reset it from the sign-in screen of the driver app with a code texted to their phone, or a fleet manager can issue a temporary PIN from the driver's page.
A driver has no mobile data
The app keeps working offline and syncs later. A driver can also text updates to {{.App.SMSNumber}}: the text sent with each assignment says what to reply.
What did the customer receive?
An administrator can see every text sent and received under Settings → Text messages.
Who changed something?
Each shipment's history lists every change and who made it. An administrator can also see sign-ins and account changes in the audit log.
