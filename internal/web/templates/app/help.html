How can we help?

Answers for dispatchers, drivers and the people who pay the bills.

{{icon "rocket"}}

Getting started

Add your first truck and driver

Under Vehicles → Add vehicle, enter the plate, the type and what it carries, and when its insurance and inspection (ቦሎ) run out. Then under Drivers → Add driver, enter the driver's name and phone and choose their usual truck. They get a PIN by SMS and sign in to the driver app with their phone number.

Book and assign a shipment

New shipment asks for the customer, where to pick up and deliver, and the load. Choose a driver now or later: the shipment appears on their phone, and the customer gets a text with a tracking link. Many shipments at once can be imported from a spreadsheet with Import CSV.

Invite your team

An administrator adds office staff under Team. Each person gets a temporary password, shown once and texted to them, and chooses their own when they first sign in.

{{icon "package"}}

Shipments and tracking

What customers see on the tracking page

The status, when the load is expected and whether it is running late, how far along the route it is, where the truck was last seen, each update so far, and your phone number to call. After delivery they can confirm they received it.

Change a shipment after it's booked

Open the shipment and choose Edit. Anything can change until it is delivered or cancelled. If the driver is affected (a new destination, say), they get a text and the app updates. Every change is kept in the shipment's history with who made it.

Proof of delivery: photos and signatures

At delivery the driver takes the receiver's name, a signature and photos in the app, even without signal; they upload when it comes back. They appear on the shipment's page, with whether the truck was at the drop-off.

Keep papers and charges with a load

A shipment's Documents tab keeps the signed waybill, customs papers and invoices (PDFs or photos, up to 10 MB). Its Charges tab lists what the customer pays, line by line; a discount is a negative line.

Drivers and the app

Get drivers on the app

On their phone, drivers open {{$app.PublicURL}}/driver in the browser and sign in with their phone number and PIN. The browser offers to add it to the home screen. There is nothing to download from an app store.

When a driver's phone goes offline

The app keeps working without signal and sends what it saved when it reconnects, with the right times. A driver who has been quiet too long while carrying a load raises an alert; you choose how long under Alerts → When we alert you.{{with $app.SMSNumber}} Without data, a driver can text updates to {{.}}: the text sent with each trip says what to reply.{{end}}

Battery and data use on the driver's phone

The app records the truck's position every minute or so while on a trip. With the battery saver, which turns on by itself below 20%, it records one every 15 minutes and syncs less often. A driver's page shows their battery and how much the app stores.

A driver forgot their PIN

They can reset it from the app's sign-in screen with a code texted to their phone, or a fleet manager can issue a temporary PIN from the driver's page.

Billing

How plans are priced

Each plan has a monthly price, a number of driver places and an allowance of texts. Texts beyond the allowance are charged per part. The Billing page shows your plan, what you have used this month, and the other plans.

Pay with telebirr, CBE Birr or a bank transfer

Open an invoice on the Billing page and choose how to pay. A bank transfer is matched by hand when it arrives, which can take a working day; the invoice shows the account.

Invoices for your accountant

Every invoice and receipt is on the Billing page, ready to print, with your TIN once it is set under Settings.

Who changed something?

Each shipment's history lists every change and who made it. An administrator can see sign-ins and account changes in the audit log, and every text sent and received under Settings → Text messages.
